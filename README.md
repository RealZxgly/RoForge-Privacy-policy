# RoForge Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 26, 2026_

RoForge is a browser extension for Roblox made by Redshift Games. This policy explains what RoForge stores, where, and why.

## What RoForge stores in your browser

These stay on your computer, in the extension's own storage:

- Your RoForge settings (which features are on or off).
- Your playtime history (which Roblox games you played and for how long), used for the playtime charts and heatmaps.
- Your Embers balance, shop items and equipped cosmetics.
- Your RoForge login session, so you stay logged in.

You can delete all of it at any time by removing the extension.

## What RoForge sends to the RoForge server

When you log in with Roblox, RoForge's server (hosted on Cloudflare) stores:

- Your Roblox user ID, username and display name.
- Your RoForge Plus plan (Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime), when it ends, and a record of your Plus purchases.
- A login session. Only a scrambled (hashed) version of the session is stored.

We do not store your Roblox password or cookies. You log in by joining the RoForge game on Roblox, and the game tells the server who you are.

## Other services RoForge talks to

- **Roblox** (roblox.com and its APIs), to show game, group, badge, server and profile information. These requests use your normal Roblox login in your browser, like the Roblox website does.
- **geojs.io**, to show the approximate region of game servers, and your own approximate location (city level, from your IP address) to estimate which servers are closest. That location is kept only in your browser.
- **GitHub** (gist.githubusercontent.com), to download the list of RoForge tags (such as "Owner" or "Tester"). Nothing about you is sent there.

## Payments

RoForge Plus is bought with Robux through game passes in the RoForge game on Roblox. Roblox handles the payment. RoForge never sees any payment details.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or share your data.
- We don't show ads or use ad trackers.
- We don't read your messages, trades or anything else not needed for RoForge's features.
